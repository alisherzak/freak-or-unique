№ 08 — Aftercare

Looking after it.

The short version, in five stages.

Leave the wrap on for 2–4 hours, or as instructed by your artist on the day. When you take it off, wash gently with lukewarm water and a fragrance-free soap. Pat dry — don't rub — then leave the skin to breathe for the rest of the evening.

Avoid alcohol, the gym, and anything that's going to make you sweat into a fresh wound. No baths, no swimming, no sun.

Wash 2–3 times a day with the same fragrance-free soap. Apply a thin layer of unscented healing balm or moisturiser whenever it feels tight — too much will smother it.

Expect mild itching, scabbing, and some colour loss as the skin sheds. This is normal. Don't pick, don't scratch, don't peel.

Surface healing takes around 2–3 weeks. Deeper healing — where the ink settles and the skin fully knits back — runs closer to 6–8 weeks. Keep it moisturised, keep it out of direct sun, and SPF 50 it for life once healed.

Some redness and warmth in the first day or two is normal. Get in touch — or see a GP — if you see spreading redness, yellow/green discharge, fever, or pain that's getting worse instead of better after the first 48 hours.

If in doubt, send us a photo. We'd rather take a look than have you wait it out.

Can I shower? Yes — quick, lukewarm, no direct stream on the tattoo. No long soaks until it's fully healed.

When can I swim? No pools, sea, or hot tubs for at least 3 weeks.

Touch-ups? Ask your artist — touch-up policy varies by piece. Send us a photo at the 4-week mark and we'll take a look.
